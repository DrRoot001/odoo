# MVP Scope

The smallest usable clone should include: identity and roles; company/tenant context; contacts/customers; products; CRM leads; quotations and sales orders; inventory availability and fulfillment; vendor purchase orders; invoices and payments; basic reporting/export; audit/error handling; and backup/restore.

MVP excludes full parity domains, every localization/provider, advanced manufacturing, complete payroll, every website/ecommerce surface, and all 666 modules. Each exclusion must be tracked in the feature parity register and accepted by product governance.
