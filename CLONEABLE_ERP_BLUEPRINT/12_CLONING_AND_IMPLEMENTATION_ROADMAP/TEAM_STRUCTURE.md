# Team Structure

A practical delivery team requires product/domain ownership, architecture, backend/domain engineers, frontend/UX engineers, data/migration engineering, QA/automation, security engineering, DevOps/SRE, and technical documentation/governance. Assign module owners and reviewers using the 666-module coverage register.

Team size, staffing duration, and productivity are not established by the source and must be planned from selected MVP scope and delivery constraints.
